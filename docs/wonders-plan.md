# Tips as Wonders

*Ten tips, and the Wonder that carries each one.*

A Wonder is a touchable full-screen visual of one real phenomenon, with the tip's words made out of
its own material where the material can carry letters. The form comes from Altitude
(`~/Documents/random/psychedelic-integration/app`, repo `trip-helper`) by way of Tao
(`~/Documents/apps/tao`), which first turned a body of text into Wonders. These ten live in Practical
Wisdom's **Animations (demo)** section. Approved by Tom on 2026-10-24.

## How the ten were chosen

Tom's rule from Tao: *"only the chapter's own pictures and the Wonder's phenomenon. I don't need any
more new analogies."* So a tip qualifies only if its own words hold a picture. Most tips are abstract
advice with nothing to show ("Satisfice, don't maximise") and are left out rather than given an
invented metaphor. Two kinds survive:

- a physical image already in the words (a pencil, a cut, the sun), and
- an idiom whose picture has gone to sleep ("swings and roundabouts", "boil it down", "polar
  opposites", "push through"). Making the idiom literal is the tip's own picture, not a new one.

Production scores run 0 to 2 across all 188 tips, so popularity could not rank them. No two of the ten
share a mechanism or a font. Left out for a reason: #130 "let go" (Altitude's dandelion already says
*"Let go, and it travels"*); #179 "slowly, as long as I don't stop" and #120 "this too will pass" (no
picture in their words).

## How each entry reads

- **The line** is the planted phrase, the tip's own words, lightly trimmed only where a symbol or
  length would not form in material.
- **The idea** is the tip's anecdote, shown as the reading. Authored by Tom, fetched live from the
  tip so an edit to it updates the Wonder. Never generated.
- **Wonder** is the phenomenon, and what it does left alone.
- **Touch** is what a finger does to it, physically.
- **Words**: *become* (the material forms the letters), *around* (the line sits in the scene and the
  material moves round it), *revealed* (the letters were there and the phenomenon uncovers them).
- **Voice** is the house font (lib/fonts.js `feel`).
- **Each time** is what the seed changes, so no visit opens the same way.
- **Watch** names the known risk.

House rules for every one: a real phenomenon, never an effect; touch does something physical; calm
(any time multiplier ≤ 0.8 rad/s); seeded per mount; nothing that reads as eyes, a face, cracks or
lightning; no stars or sparkles; dark ground; canvas 2D, ≤ 30 fps, a slower 10 fps version for
reduced motion that still answers touch.

## 1. Pencil — tip #39 — `pw-pencil`

- **The line:** The bluntest pencil is sharper than the sharpest memory.
- **The idea:** *(this tip has no anecdote; the reading is the tip itself)*
- **Wonder:** a sheet of paper lit from the side, close enough that its tooth shows. The line is
  written across it in soft graphite, stroke by stroke, the grain catching where the lead skipped over
  the paper's high points.
- **Touch:** your finger writes in pencil too. Whatever you write stays.
- **Words:** *become* (the graphite writes them).
- **Voice:** Caveat.
- **Each time:** the paper's tooth, its warmth, and the angle of the light.
- **Watch:** white paper is a large empty field. Keep it toned, textured and side-lit so the surface
  itself is the subject.

## 2. Reduction — tip #86 — `pw-reduction`

- **The line:** Boil things down to their essence.
- **The idea:** Keep drilling down till I get to the bare-bones, nutshell essence of everything. Try to
  sum up things in just a phrase or even just one word.
- **Wonder:** a shallow pan seen from above at a rolling boil. Bubbles rise and burst, steam lifts off,
  and the liquid visibly reduces.
- **Touch:** turns the heat up where you press; the boil quickens there.
- **Words:** *revealed* (as the liquid boils away, the letters are what is left, a glossy reduction on
  the pan's base).
- **Voice:** Cormorant.
- **Each time:** the liquid's colour, the pan's tone, where the boil starts.
- **Watch:** bubbles from above can pair into eyes. Keep the boil busy and mixed in size, never paired.

## 3. Filings — tip #67 — `pw-filings`

- **The line:** Polar opposite views can both be true.
- **The idea:** Sometimes it seems for every wise proverb there is, there's another that urges you to do
  the opposite. This doesn't mean the proverbs are wrong; it means I have to use judgement in knowing
  when each case applies.
- **Wonder:** iron filings scattered on paper, two magnetic poles beneath it just off the left and right
  edges. The filings align into the field running between them, one pole as true as the other.
- **Touch:** drag to move a pole; the whole field swings round to stay true to both.
- **Words:** *become* (the filings line up into the letters).
- **Voice:** Instrument.
- **Each time:** the scatter, the poles' strengths and heights, the paper.
- **Watch:** two round poles side by side are two eyes. Both stay off-screen; only the field shows.

## 4. Kerf — tip #20 — `pw-kerf`

- **The line:** Measure twice, cut once.
- **The idea:** It takes little time to double-check in order to avoid an error that would be costly to
  fix.
- **Wonder:** a timber board in raking light, grain running across it.
- **Touch:** the first touch strikes a fine pencil tick along the board, the second a second tick, and
  only the third cuts: the slot opens along the marks and sawdust spills from it. The order of the
  touches is the tip.
- **Words:** *revealed* (the sawdust falls away from them).
- **Voice:** Josefin.
- **Each time:** the species and grain of the wood, the light.
- **Watch:** never show the saw (clip-art). Only wood, marks and dust.

## 5. Swing — tip #122 — `pw-swing`

- **The line:** Life is swings and roundabouts.
- **The idea:** Sometimes I win; sometimes I lose, and often it's a bit random. When the lucky breaks
  happen, count my blessings. When bad luck strikes, console myself that good luck may be just around
  the corner.
- **Wonder:** one swing, seen side-on, moving through its arc at dusk. It trades height for speed and
  back: slow at the top of each arc, fast through the bottom.
- **Touch:** a push sends it higher, and the higher it climbs the longer it hangs at the top. What it
  gains in height it loses in speed, and gets back on the way down.
- **Words:** *around* (the swing passes through the line).
- **Voice:** Syne.
- **Each time:** the dusk colour, the swing's length, the starting arc.
- **Watch:** from the front, a seat on two chains reads as a face. Side-on only.

## 6. Threshold — tip #181 — `pw-threshold`

- **The line:** As soon as I wake up, go outside into the sun.
- **The idea:** Exposing my eyes to 5 to 10 minutes of bright sunlight immediately on waking helps boost
  my energy, mood and alertness, by resetting my circadian rhythm from sleep to wakefulness.
- **Wonder:** a doorway at dawn: low gold light laying a widening wedge across a dark floor, dust and
  morning air lit inside it.
- **Touch:** the door swings wider and the light floods further in.
- **Words:** *around* (the light falls across them).
- **Voice:** Outfit.
- **Each time:** the sun's height and warmth, the door's starting gap.
- **Watch:** Altitude has several light Wonders; this one is defined by the door and the threshold.

## 7. Stalagmite — tip #12 — `pw-stalagmite`

- **The line:** Big success is tiny successes building on each other.
- **The idea:** Don't be sad about how little I achieve in a day. As long as accomplishments head in the
  right direction and I keep going, success snowballs. Consistency is key: keep notching up small wins.
- **Wonder:** a stalagmite in lamplight. A drop falls every few seconds and leaves a thin ring of
  mineral where it lands.
- **Touch:** lets a drop fall now.
- **Words:** *become* (the letters build up ring on ring, never all at once).
- **Voice:** Fell.
- **Each time:** the drip rate, the mineral's colour, the lamp.
- **Watch:** a dark cave is a small subject in an empty field. Light it close and fill the frame.

## 8. Shoot — tip #65 — `pw-shoot`

- **The line:** Keep going. Push through.
- **The idea:** What is a magic secret to success? Consistency. To commit to a goal and to keep going.
- **Wonder:** a green shoot pushing up through soft snow, which slumps aside as it rises.
- **Touch:** gives it another push upward.
- **Words:** *become* (the green rises into the letters).
- **Voice:** Playfair.
- **Each time:** the snow's depth and light, where the shoot comes up.
- **Watch:** a crust that splits reads as cracks (a house rule). Snow that slumps, never fractures.

## 9. Bloom — tip #3 — `pw-bloom`

- **The line:** Help others to flourish.
- **The idea:** If I want to increase my positive multifold, invest some of my time and resources in
  helping others to flourish.
- **Wonder:** a bed of seedlings in soft light.
- **Touch:** the plants *around* the touch open into flower, not the one under the finger. You help;
  others flourish.
- **Words:** *revealed* (the blooms fill in the letters).
- **Voice:** Fraunces.
- **Each time:** the flowers' colour and where the bed is thick.
- **Watch:** the second growing thing in the set: #8 is one shoot forcing upward, this is a field
  opening outward. No dark-centred flowers, which read as eyes.

## 10. Petal — tip #107 — `pw-petal`

- **The line:** Whenever my mind wanders off, bring it back.
- **The idea:** The more times I catch my mind wandering and successfully bring it back, the more
  successful the meditation session.
- **Wonder:** a still pool with one petal floating on it. The water's own surface tension draws a
  floating thing toward the centre.
- **Touch:** sends a ripple that pushes the petal away; it drifts off and, unforced, comes back.
- **Words:** *around* (the line floats where the petal returns).
- **Voice:** Shippori.
- **Each time:** the petal's colour and shape, the light on the water.
- **Watch:** keep the petal off the line while it is read.

## Build order

Pilot first, the three riskiest families: **pencil, reduction, filings**. Review them at phone size
(deviceScaleFactor 2.625) before building the other seven, so what that review catches carries into
the rest rather than being repeated seven times.
